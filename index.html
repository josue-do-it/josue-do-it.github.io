About

I am Josué Romaric Edou, from Benin, West Africa. I am a Google DeepMind Scholar, early in my research career in artificial intelligence, and I am looking for a PhD position. My research interests are computer vision, large language models and robotics, and machine learning with applications spanning visual perception and generative models.

I recently completed my MSc in Artificial Intelligence for Science at the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) in Cape Town, South Africa. My thesis focused on model-free, open-vocabulary 6D pose estimation. The idea is simple: we developed a pipeline that detects an object it has never seen before and estimates its 6D pose from a natural-language description. The object is first extracted from the human context using Llama 3.2. The pipeline is used for robotic grasping. Thesis →

Before my MSc, I earned an engineer's degree in Mathematical Engineering and Modelling in Benin, which gave me a strong mathematical foundation and practical experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow and scientific computing. Before starting my research career, I worked as an AI Engineer in Benin. I am open to research opportunities!
